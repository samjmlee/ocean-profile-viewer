How to read this view. Points show observed display bins (up to 1,200 profiles × 180 depths sampled for rendering); the curtain joins neighboring observed profiles; gaps and missing cells stay open. Each profile belongs to its own time. Depth is derived from sea pressure and latitude using TEOS-10. The globe is a geographic reference; no bathymetry, ice thickness, water velocity or unobserved 3D ocean volume is inferred. Native cast replay interpolates between retained time–pressure samples and does not track a water parcel.
Display methods

The same imported observations are used in 2D and 3D. This view bins the selected phase into the workspace-selected pressure-bin medians for rendering. Missing bins and temporal gaps remain gaps. Source coordinates locate the surface/platform track, not a measured underwater trajectory. No bathymetry is implied.
